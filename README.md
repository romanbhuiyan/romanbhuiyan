## 📌 About Me
I am a **Lecturer in Computer Science and Informatics** at **BSBI**, with a strong background in **artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, and deep learning**. I earned my **Ph.D. in Information Technology** from **Multimedia University, Malaysia**, where I focused on visual data analysis using computer vision and deep learning techniques. I also completed my **postdoctoral fellowship** at **Fraunhofer IGD**, Rostock, Germany (Nov 2022 – Oct 2023), where I led a **smart farming** project for **chicken behaviour analysis** using deep learning, achieving **state-of-the-art results**.

With experience spanning **academia** and **industry**, I combine theoretical research expertise with practical software engineering knowledge to drive interdisciplinary research and teaching.
